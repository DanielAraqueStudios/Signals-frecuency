# Audio sample sources & attribution

Recordings used for the item 8 animal-sound analysis (`report/figuras/gato.png`,
`perro.png`, `gallo.png`, `animales_comparacion.png`), downloaded from Wikimedia
Commons. Kept in this repo (an exception to the general `audio_samples/`
gitignore rule) because they are small, freely licensed, and needed to
reproduce the report's figures.

| Category | File | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gato | `gato/gato.ogg` | [Meow domestic cat.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow_domestic_cat.ogg) | Smser | GFDL |
| Perro | `perro/perro.ogg` | [Barking of a dog.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Barking_of_a_dog.ogg) | Amada44 | CC BY-SA 3.0 |
| Gallo | `gallo/gallo.ogg` | [Rooster crowing.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rooster_crowing.ogg) | Wikimedia Commons contributor | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Instrument (contrabajo/piano/flauta) and voice (persona 1/2) recordings referenced
in `report/secciones/metodologia.tex` have not been sourced yet — those figures
and their table rows in `report/secciones/resultados.tex` remain the original
placeholder values from `sample.tex` until real recordings are added the same way.
